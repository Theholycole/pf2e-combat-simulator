# Morale: decisions

Places where `Improved Morale.md` and `Morale.md` disagree, are silent, or can't be applied literally by a simulator. Each has a default so the engine can run. **The user confirmed every default on 2026-10-08** (M2 and M19 were already their own decisions). Every default is a data entry in `src/engine/morale-rules.js`, so changing it is a one-line edit or a world-setting override.

| # | Question | Default chosen | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| M1 | Frightened lowers both the check (d20 + Will) and the threshold (10 + Will), which would cancel it out. | The threshold uses Will **without** condition penalties; the check takes them. The separate "−1 currently frightened" modifier also applies. | Your design goal says frightened is "the main visible morale pressure", which can't work if it cancels. |
| M2 | Should morale checks be limited to fights the party is already winning? | **Decided by the user (2026-10-07): no. Morale is always active.** The triggers themselves (leader lost, can't affect an opponent, and so on) are the signs of losing. An optional **losing gate** remains in the rules (`losingGate`, off): when on, checks happen only while party HP × damage per round is at least `losingGateRatio` (3) × the foes'. | Measured over 20 benchmark fights (levels 1–7, 800 runs each). **Always active** (re-measured after the planner and M12 fixes on 2026-10-07): morale raises the party's win rate by up to **14.9 points** in close fights (level 7 Extreme +14.9; level 3 Extreme +11.8; level 5 Extreme +11.5; level 7 Severe +8.4; boss fights +6 to +8). Won fights get up to 1.45 rounds shorter, except boss fights, which get slightly longer: a wavering boss fights more defensively. Before the fixes the biggest change was +24.5. **With the gate at 3:** at most 2.6 points; 0.42 rounds shorter on average. The effect is spread across many triggers. The biggest (average over four close fights) are: sees its strongest ally destroyed +3.7, falls to half HP +3.5, surrounded +2.3, side falls to half strength +2.0. |
| M3 | `Morale.md` (AD&D) uses 2d10 against fixed creature-type numbers. | Not used as the engine. Its extra situational modifiers are data entries, **disabled by default**: hated enemy +4, fighting magic users −2, outnumber 3:1 +2, outnumbered 3:1 −4, most powerful ally killed −4, no enemy slain −2, abandoned −6. | You chose `Improved Morale.md` as the engine. |
| M4 | A Group (mob) failing moves one state. What does a critical failure do? | Failure moves 1 state; critical failure moves 2. | Mirrors the individual waver/break split. |
| M5 | When is a set of foes a Group? | 3 or more with the same name. | The note says "mobs, squads, and swarms"; two creatures check individually. |
| M6 | Leader bonus for groups (+1 sergeant … +3 tyrant). | Leader level − group level: 1–2 → +1, 3–4 → +2, 5+ → +3. | The note's titles can't be read from a stat block. |
| M7 | Which break result a creature uses. | Cowardly, Bestial and Typical non-humanoid → **rout**. Typical humanoid and Mercenary → **collapse** (surrender). Disciplined → **disordered retreat**. Fanatical → **berserk**. Boss → **rout**. | The note says "choose one"; this picks by template. |
| M8 | Which waver result. | Every waver gives frightened 1 if not already frightened, and then: Cowardly → withdraw (must spend an action moving away), Disciplined → defensive posture, everyone else → defensive posture plus drop aggression (prefers defence). | The note says "pick the most sensible option". |
| M9 | "Rout: fleeing for 1 round." | A fleeing creature moves away. If it reaches *far*, it leaves the fight (agreed in the interview). | A creature at far with fleeing has no reason to come back in an abstract fight. |
| M10 | First-member trigger: "to a critical hit, spell, or obviously overwhelming attack". | A critical hit, or a single hit dealing at least 50% of the victim's max HP. Spells join in milestone 2. | Makes "overwhelming" measurable. |
| M11 | "Can't meaningfully affect an opponent." | Best hit chance against every active enemy is 10% or less, or best expected damage per Strike is under 1 after resistances. | Makes it measurable. |
| M12 | "Surrounded." | Engaged by 3 or more active enemies who also outnumber the creature's side in that Melee by at least 2. | No grid; the Melee count stands in. An abstract Melee engages everyone with everyone, so a 4-PC brawl made every foe "surrounded" and set off checks in fights that were still even (seen in sample logs, 2026-10-07). |
| M13 | "Isolated from allies" (−4). | No active ally is engaged or near. | Same. |
| M14 | Situational settings that the simulator can't observe: defending home (+1), no retreat / surrender means death (+2), unpaid or betrayed mercenary (−2), Bestial defending young/lair (+2). | Per-run checkboxes in the setup dialog, off by default. | Only the GM knows. |
| M15 | Boss Resolve: "objective clearly lost." | The party's objective has succeeded. | That is the boss's side losing its objective. |
| M16 | Recovery: "can see at least one ally still fighting". | At least one active ally is not wavering or fleeing. | Approximates "sees" without line of sight. |
| M17 | PCs never check morale. NPC allies on the party's side? | They don't check in milestone 1. | The note allows it, but party-side NPCs are rare; it's a toggle for later. |
| M18 | "Bloodied" for Bestial (−2 once bloodied and escape is possible). | At or below half HP and not engaged by 3 or more enemies. | Makes it measurable. |
| M19 | Can "falls to half HP" and "sees its strongest ally destroyed" trigger more than once? | **Decided by the user (2026-10-07): once per creature per fight** (`oncePerCreature`). | In milestone 1 this changed nothing (identical results on six close fights): with no healing a creature crosses half HP only once, and there is only one strongest ally. It matters from milestone 2, when healing can lift a creature back above half HP. |
| M20 | What morale is for: shorten fights the party is winning, with little effect on the win rate, unless the players play for morale. First step: drop the "falls to half HP" trigger. | **Decided by the user (2026-10-08): the trigger is off** (`triggers.halfHp.enabled: false`; the −1 "at half HP" modifier stays for checks other triggers start). Morale stays always active. | On the 12 benchmark fights (levels 1, 5, 10 and 14, moderate to extreme, 200 fights each, morale on against off): the win rate morale adds went from **+5.2 to +3.3** points (fights the party wins easily +1.6 → +0.9, close fights +10.2 → +6.7). Rounds saved in won fights went from 0.72 to 0.44, and foes who flee or give up from 22% to 13%. Of the rules left, "sees its strongest ally destroyed" adds the most win rate (+1.0) for almost no rounds (+0.03). "Isolated" (+0.25 rounds, +0.2) and "outnumbered 2:1" (+0.18 rounds, +0.2) save the most rounds for their win rate. |
| M21 | Next steps toward M20's aim. | **Decided by the user (2026-10-08):** "sees its strongest ally destroyed" is off; "isolated from allies" is −6 (was −4) and "side outnumbered 2:1 or worse" −4 (was −2). "Has not yet taken damage" (+2) stays. | Measured as in M20 (300 fights each), from M20's rules: dropping "strongest ally" took the win rate morale adds from +2.9 to +1.9 for 0.04 rounds. The stronger isolated and outnumbered modifiers saved 0.33 more rounds for +0.3 points; both mean the party is winning. Together: **0.78 rounds saved (was 0.49 after M20, 0.72 before it), +2.0 points (+0.4 in fights the party wins easily, +4.1 in close ones), 18% of foes flee or give up.** Also dropping "not yet taken damage" would have given 0.91 rounds at +2.5 points. |
| M22 | Which rules still decide close fights (the party wins under 80% with morale off: level 5 extreme, level 10 and 14 severe and extreme)? | **Decided by the user (2026-10-08): "side loses its first member to a critical hit or overwhelming attack" is off.** | After M21, morale added +4.1 points in close fights. Of that, this trigger was +2.1 (one early critical kill made the whole side check), "half strength" +1.1, "surrounded" +0.6; in close fights foes surrendered (8%) but never routed or fled. With it off: **0.67 rounds saved, +1.0 point (+0.3 in fights the party wins easily, +2.0 in close ones), 17% of foes flee or give up.** Also dropping "surrounded" would have given 0.61 rounds at +0.8. A bonus for foes whose side is ahead (party HP × damage per round below theirs) changed nothing even at +6: when a check comes, their side has usually just lost someone. The parked "Fit morale" tool was dropped: values alone barely move the win rate; which triggers exist does. **Playing for morale:** fear from the party still sets off a check in any fight, but the simulated PCs value Demoralize only by frightened's penalty, so a party able to Demoralize used it 0.01 times a fight and morale's effect didn't change. |
| M23 | M20's exception: players who play for morale on purpose. | **Decided by the user (2026-10-08): a "The party plays for morale" tick in the setup** (`setup.situational.playsForMorale`, off by default). With it, a PC values a fear action (anything that makes a foe frightened) by the chance the check it sets off makes the foe waver or break, and values the foes' leader by the breaks its fall would bring to the rest. **Fixed on the way:** the "frightened by an enemy effect" trigger was never raised once spells and actions took over applying conditions; frightened from an enemy's effect now raises it. | On the 12 benchmark fights, a party that can Demoralize (Intimidation level + 7). **Without the tick:** it Demoralizes 0.01 times a fight, and morale saves 0.70 rounds and adds +1.1 points, the same as a party that can't. **With the tick:** it Demoralizes 5.5 times a fight, 62% of the checks it sets off fail, and morale saves **1.09 rounds and adds +8.0 points (+17.7 in close fights); 37% of foes flee or give up.** Playing for morale pays off; not playing for it leaves morale small. The bestiary sweeps have 0 errors. |
| M24 | A new morale system for M20's aim, from the user's "Morale.md" and "Improved Morale.md" ("cleanup morale"). Supersedes M2's "always active" and M1's threshold. | **Decided by the user (2026-10-08).** **When:** only once the creature's side is losing: 25%, 50% and 75% of the side fallen; a companion slain after half have fallen; the leader slain or fled; can't harm the enemy; surrounded; outnumbered 3:1. Plus fear from the party. (The early triggers, surprised and help arriving, are off.) **The gate is on at 2×:** checks only while party HP × damage per round is at least twice the foes'; fear from the party counts anyway, and "The party plays for morale" lifts the gate. **The check:** d20 + its Will + modifiers against PF2e's DC for the party's level (offset 0), −1 per level below the party (+1 above); −2 once a quarter of its side has fallen, −4 from half. **A failure means it gives up** (flees or surrenders by its template; fanatics fight recklessly instead; Boss Resolve holds). No wavering, no recovery, no group checks; the rout at half stays. | Measured morale on against off, 300 fights each. **Equal-level benchmarks** (levels 1, 5, 10, 14 at moderate, severe and extreme): **1.79 rounds saved, +0.8 points (+0.3 easy, +1.5 close)**; was 0.67 and +1.0. **Wider set** (levels 1, 5, 10 with weak groups and +3/+4 bosses): 1.05 rounds, +0.4. Foes 2 levels below the party give up 63% of the time, equal ones 35%, bosses ~0%. Biggest single fight: level 5 extreme +4.2 points (2.9 rounds saved); the rest within ±1.7. The gate at 3× gave 1.51 rounds for +0.2. Without a gate the best was 1.02 rounds for +2.1, past the +1 limit. A party that Demoralizes: +1.2 points without the tick, **+22.9 with it** (+51.9 in close fights), 2.22 rounds. |
| M25 | Playing for morale swung close fights by +51.9 points (a Demoralizing party with the tick): every Demoralize that landed forced a check, and with M24 a failure gives up. | **Decided by the user (2026-10-08): fear forces a morale check only when it takes a foe to frightened 2 or more** (`triggers.frightenedByEnemy.minValue: 2`): a Demoralize's critical success, stronger fear, or abilities that raise frightened. A plain Demoralize success (frightened 1) no longer does. | Same Demoralizing party, 12 equal-level benchmarks. **With the tick:** it Demoralizes 0.7–2.1 times a fight, fear checks fall to about 0.1 a fight (a critical Demoralize needs a natural 20 against these foes), and morale adds +11.2 points (+25.4 close, was +51.9), 1.88 rounds. Almost all of what's left comes from the tick lifting the gate for the side-losing checks. **Without the tick:** +0.9 points (+1.7 close), 1.79 rounds, the same as a party that doesn't Demoralize. The tick's gate is now a setting (`playsForMoraleGateRatio`; 0 lifts it): at 1× +5.3 (+11.3 close), at 1.5× +2.7 (+5.0 close), left at 2× +1.4 (+2.7 close). |
| M26 | Should "The party plays for morale" change the rules (lift or lower the gate)? | **Decided by the user (2026-10-08): no.** "In real play I can't tell if players are playing for morale; there shouldn't be different rules for playing into it or not." The rules are the same for every party. The tick now only changes how the simulated PCs act (they value fear by the chance it makes a foe give up, and go for the foes' leader); its label says so: "Simulated PCs play for morale". | With the gate unchanged, the Demoralizing party adds +1.4 points (+2.7 in close fights) with the tick, +0.9 without (M25). |
| M27 | When the gate blocks a check, is it lost, or held and rolled once the gate opens? | **Decided by the user (2026-10-08): lost** (`holdGatedChecks: false`; holding stays as a setting). | Held, on the 12 equal-level benchmarks: 1.85 rounds saved (lost: 1.79), +1.5 points (lost: +0.8), +2.9 in close fights (lost: +1.5). In a fight the party is losing, held checks pile up and fire together when the party briefly gets ahead: level 10 severe went from +0.5 to +4.3 points. Wider set: 1.06 rounds and +0.5 (lost: 1.05, +0.4). The gate's strength measure is HP × a forecast of each creature's damage next round from its stat block (its best Strike over three attacks, the third at half weight, or its best castable damage spell), recomputed every round; not the damage dealt so far. |
| M28 | How the gate tells the party is winning: the damage forecast (HP × damage per round, M24) or each side's share of its starting HP? | **Decided by the user (2026-10-08): HP share**, simple to judge at the table. The party's HP left as a share of what it started with must be at least **1.25×** the foes' (`gateMode: "hpShare"`, `hpGate: { kind: "ratio", value: 1.25 }`); creatures down, fled or surrendered count as none. The damage forecast stays as a setting (`gateMode: "strength"`). | Equal-level / wider benchmarks, 300 fights each. **HP share 1.25×: 1.53 / 0.91 rounds saved, +0.5 / +0.2 points (+1.1 in close fights).** Damage forecast 2×: 1.79 / 1.05, +0.8 / +0.4 (+1.5). Other HP-share settings: party just ahead (1×) 1.85 rounds, +3.2 (+7.3 close); 10 points ahead 1.61, +1.2; 20 points ahead 1.22, +0.2; 1.5× 1.17, +0.1; 2× 0.52, +0.0. At the same win-rate effect HP share saves about 0.2 rounds fewer: it can't see a party that's ahead on HP but outclassed.
