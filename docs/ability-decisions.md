# Abilities: decisions

Milestone 3, first slice: creatures use the basic actions and their monster abilities in the simulation. The ability module (`pf2e-automatic-ability-resolution`) compiles them from PF2e's data, the core resolves them (the `check` family for skill actions, `save` for breath weapons and the like), and `src/engine/spells.js` plays them alongside spells. **The user confirmed B1–B6 on 2026-10-08, and B7–B11 (the other skill actions) later the same day.** B12–B21 (more monster abilities, added the same day) were confirmed later that day too. B22–B26 (reactions, confused, Retch, charges) were confirmed the same day. B27–B28 (Strike activities, Engulf) were confirmed the same day.

| # | Question | Default chosen | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| B1 | Who uses the basic actions (Demoralize, Trip, Grapple, Shove, Escape)? | Every PC. A monster uses one only if its stat block lists the skill (Athletics for Trip, Grapple and Shove; Intimidation for Demoralize). Anyone can Escape. Monsters also use the riders their Strikes list. | A stat block's skills show what the creature is built to do. |
| B2 | Frightful Presence and other auras that make creatures save. | Each enemy in range saves once when the fight starts, then is immune. From 60 feet, that's everyone; nearer, not those in the far band. | "A creature that first enters the area". Bands can't track entering an aura later. |
| B3 | Recharge ("can't use it again for 1d4 rounds"). | Roll it when the ability is used; it's available again at the start of the creature's turn that many rounds later. | PF2e's usual reading. |
| B4 | Riders (Grab, Knockdown, Push). | The Grapple, Trip or Shove the Strike allows, with no multiple attack penalty. It can only be the action straight after the hit; Improved ones happen at once, for free. A creature grabbed or restrained is held until the end of the grabber's next turn, unless the grabber moves or the creature Escapes. | PF2e's glossary and Grapple's own text. |
| B5 | Rend and Ferocity. | Rend: the listed Strike's damage again, after two hits with it on the same enemy this round. Ferocity: once per round, a monster reduced to 0 HP stays at 1 with wounded +1, until wounded 3; massive damage still kills. | PF2e's glossary. |
| B6 | How conditions and buffs compare to damage in planning. | **Tuned on 2026-10-08:** conditions and spell effects (measured in rounds of a creature's threat) count for a third, next to damage (measured as a share of HP, where a kill counts once). A fight has about three rounds left, so a round of threat is about a third of a kill. Without this, the martial party Grappled about 5½ times a fight instead of Striking. | Keeps the S7 weights, which the user confirmed as estimates "to tune as results come in". |
| B7 | Disarm. | Only a weapon in hand can be knocked away, never a natural attack (jaws, claws, a tail or another unarmed Strike). A critical success drops the creature's best held weapon; picking it up is an Interact (1 action, which can provoke a Reactive Strike). A success gives −2 to attacks with it until the creature Interacts to regrip (1 action), and +2 to further Disarms against it. A creature uses Disarm if it's trained in Athletics. | PF2e's remastered text: the weakened grip "lasts as long as the creature holds the item". The free hand and size limits aren't checked. |
| B8 | Feint. | Only against a creature it's in melee with. Critical success: the target is off-guard to the feinter's melee attacks until the end of the feinter's next turn. Success: off-guard to its next melee attack this turn. Critical failure: the feinter is off-guard to the target's melee attacks until the end of its next turn. Other creatures' attacks aren't helped. | PF2e's text, with off-guard tracked per attacker. |
| B9 | Create a Diversion. | One action against every enemy within 30 feet (up to 6). Each one whose Perception DC it beats is off-guard to the creature's next attack this turn. It's tried once per enemy per fight: the +4 to their DCs for a minute makes another try rarely worth an action. The check is rolled separately for each watcher, though PF2e rolls one Deception check for them all; the expected result is the same. | The "hidden" condition matters here only for the next Strike; the simulator doesn't play Sneaking away. |
| B10 | Administer First Aid. | Only to stabilize a dying PC (stopping bleeding comes later). It takes 2 actions, and anyone can reach the downed PC, as for healing spells. A PC needs a healer's toolkit in their inventory. A monster trained in Medicine is assumed to carry one. Success or critical success: the PC loses dying but stays unconscious at 0 HP. Critical failure: dying +1. The planner weighs it by the chance the PC would otherwise die. | PF2e's text; downed monsters don't get back up in the simulator, so they aren't treated. |
| B11 | Reposition. | Compiled but never chosen: the target must stay within the actor's reach, and a 5- or 10-foot move inside a Melee changes nothing in range bands. | Bands don't track squares. Live-play cards can move the token later. |
| B12 | Swallow Whole. | Only a creature the monster holds, with an Athletics check against its Reflex DC. A creature of the listed size fills the monster; it holds two one size smaller, four smaller still (PF2e leaves the number to the GM). The swallowed creature is out of everyone else's reach: no one targets it and areas miss it. It's grabbed and slowed 1, and takes the listed damage when swallowed and at the end of each of its turns. It tries to Escape with each action, unless a hit with an unarmed attack or a light weapon would cut it free. It gets out by Escaping (against the monster's Athletics DC), when one attack or spell deals the monster piercing or slashing damage of at least the Rupture value, or when the monster drops, dies or leaves. | PF2e's glossary, except two shortcuts: getting out of a dead monster takes 3 actions in PF2e (here it's at once), and holding breath isn't tracked (fights are short). If every PC is swallowed or down, the fight counts as lost. |
| B13 | Trample. | 3 actions: the enemies of the listed size or smaller in one Melee (or half of those standing apart nearby) each attempt a basic Reflex save against the listed Strike's damage. The monster ends in their Melee; the Stride can provoke reactions. | In bands, a path through one group. |
| B14 | Regeneration and Fast Healing. | Both heal at the start of the monster's turn. With Regeneration working, a monster at 0 HP is dying (never past 3) rather than dead, and gets back up at the start of its turn (prone, wounded +1). Damage of a listed type turns it off until the end of the monster's next turn; then damage while it's down kills it. A downed regenerating monster counts as beaten when the fight ends (the party finishes it off afterwards). Fast Healing with a condition ("while underground") isn't applied. | PF2e's glossary. Materials (cold iron, silver) aren't tracked on Strikes, so they never turn Regeneration off. |
| B15 | Sneak Attack, Pack Attack and other extra Strike damage. | Sneak Attack's dice against an off-guard target (flanked, prone, grabbed, feinted and so on); Pack Attack's when two of the monster's allies are in the target's Melee; other dice against the living, the undead or the frightened. Doubled on a critical hit. | Read from the abilities' own rules (PF2e's DamageDice). |
| B16 | Draconic Frenzy and Draconic Momentum. | "Makes two Claw Strikes and one Horn Strike" (2 actions): all at one target (another enemy in reach if it drops), agile Strikes last for the multiple attack penalty. Draconic Momentum: a critical hit with any Strike recharges the named breath weapon. | PF2e's text. |
| B17 | Pounce. | Stride and Strike as one action, when one Stride reaches the target; its best melee Strike. Charges with extra effects (Powerful Charge's bonus damage) aren't played yet. | PF2e's text. |
| B18 | "+1 status to all saves vs. magic" and the like. | Against spells (all magical) and abilities with the magical trait or a tradition's trait. Bonuses against a trait (mental, fear) apply to sources with that trait. The highest bonus of each type counts. | Read from the abilities' own rules (PF2e's FlatModifier with its predicate). |
| B19 | Stench. | As B2: each enemy in range saves once (Fortitude) when the fight starts. Failure: sickened 1. Critical failure: also slowed 1, for as long as it's sickened. Sickened wears off only by Retching (B25). | PF2e's glossary. |
| B20 | Creature afflictions (poisons, diseases). | A Strike that lists one exposes the creature it hits, for free. Fortitude save: a failure is stage 1, a critical failure stage 2. Each stage deals its damage and sets its conditions. When a stage's duration runs out (at the start of the poisoner's turn), a new save: −2 stages on a critical success, −1 on a success, +1 on a failure, +2 on a critical failure. At stage 0 it's over, and the maximum duration ends it too. Another exposure to a poison it already has raises the stage by 1 (2 on a critical failure) without resetting the maximum; a disease or curse it already has does nothing. Stages measured in hours or days (most diseases) give their first stage only, and an onset of a minute or more means nothing happens in the fight. A final stage that says "dead" kills. Listed immunities (poison, disease) prevent it. The planner counts a venom when choosing which Strike to use. | PF2e's affliction rules. |
| B21 | Saves written as prose ("must succeed at a Will save or become stupefied 2 (stupefied 4 on a critical failure)"; "A creature that fails its save is slowed 1 for 1 round"). | A failure gets the conditions or damage the sentence names. A critical failure gets the stated replacement, the stated addition, or the failure's own. Damage dealt in the save's own sentence ("dealing 6d6 mental damage in a cone (Will save)") is treated as a basic save: halved on a success. Saves that follow a Strike's hit, or a creature's death, aren't offered as actions. Auras that trigger "when a creature ends its turn in the aura" are saved against once when the fight starts, as in B2. | Monster Core's usual wording. The breath weapons' damage has no "basic" mark there, but it reads as one. |
| B22 | Defensive reactions. | Nimble Dodge and the like (+AC "against the triggering attack" when targeted) are used on the first Strike at the monster, unless it has Reactive Strike to keep its reaction for. Retract Body and the like (+AC once it's hit) are used only when the bonus turns the hit aside. Challenge Fate (the attacker rolls twice and takes the worse) is used on the first Strike at it. Only Strikes trigger them for now, not spell attacks. Other parts of an effect (Ghost Dodge's resistances) aren't played. | The reaction's own rule (PF2e's FlatModifier on AC) and text. |
| B23 | Twisting Tail and Death Frenzy. | Twisting Tail: when an enemy in reach Strides out of the Melee or Stands, a Strike with the listed weapon at the listed penalty. A hit stops the move. Stepping doesn't trigger it, as in PF2e. Death Frenzy and the like: as the monster drops to 0 HP, it Strikes the creature that dropped it (or another enemy in reach) with the listed weapon. | PF2e's text. |
| B24 | Confused. | Off-guard, and no reactions. Each action is a Strike at a random creature in reach, friend or foe; with none in reach, a ranged Strike at a random creature, or a Stride to one. Damage from an attack or spell gives a DC 11 flat check to end it. The planner counts it as 0.8 rounds of the creature's threat per round. | PF2e's condition. |
| B25 | Retch. | A sickened creature can spend an action on a Fortitude save against the DC of what sickened it: −1 sickened on a success, −2 on a critical success. The planner values each point it might lose at a quarter of a round of the creature's threat. | PF2e's sickened condition. |
| B26 | Charges with a bonus for moving 20 feet. | The bonus always applies (Boar Charge's +2 to the attack roll, Powerful Charge's bigger damage): a charge always starts outside the Melee, 20 feet or more away in range bands. Charges with other effects (a save after the hit, a push) aren't played yet. | PF2e's text. |
| B27 | Strike activities (a Strike, perhaps after Strides, with more on a hit: Wrestle, Intimidating Strike, Combat Grab, Shield Push, Rhinoceros Charge). | On a hit: the listed conditions (the critical ones on a critical hit), push, extra damage (doubled on a critical hit) or save, for creatures of the listed size or smaller. With Strides, only from outside the target's Melee, like Pounce. Requirements: a creature it holds, a prone target, or a free hand (none while it holds a creature); held weapons aren't tracked. "Once per day" activities are used once a fight. The planner adds what the hit brings, times the chance to hit. Activities with other effects (dragging the target along, a longer reach, several targets) aren't played. | PF2e's text, read sentence by sentence; an ability with a sentence the reader doesn't know is left out. |
| B28 | Engulf. | As Trample (B13), for creatures of the monster's size or smaller. Each attempts a Reflex save, and on a failure is engulfed: as Swallow Whole (B12), escaping against the listed Escape DC, and cut free by the listed Rupture. A creature can be engulfed with others; there's no room limit. | PF2e's glossary. |

**Not yet:** Stop Bleeding (Administer First Aid's other use), reactions with other triggers (Retaliatory Strike, Avenging Bite), defensive reactions against spell attacks, Strike activities with other effects (dragging, longer reach, several targets), the fascinated and controlled conditions, class features, consumables, and live-play confirmation cards.

**Fixed with B12–B20:** Improved Grab, Improved Knockdown and Improved Push never fired from real stat blocks. The Strike lists "improved-grab", and the rider only looked for "grab".

**Measured** (`tools/bestiary-abilities.mjs`, 2026-10-08, with B7–B21):
- Setup: 1,106 creatures from Monster Core 1–2 and NPC Core, built from their real stat blocks with their abilities; 665 of them have abilities the simulation plays (548 before B12–B21).
- 3,318 fights in 11 seconds, with no errors.
- Per 3,318 fights: Grapple 2,584, Disarm 1,387, Trip 972, Demoralize 630, Create a Diversion 375, Escape 372, Improved Grab 342, Administer First Aid 164, Feint 158, Constrict 142, Frightful Presence 123, Swallow Whole 120 (76 creatures swallowed), Draconic Frenzy 87, Grab 86, Ferocity 64, Trample 58, Draconic Momentum 57 recharges, Pounce 26, plus dozens of breath weapons and other one-off abilities.
- Afflictions: 292 stages reached, 101 stage saves, 16 recoveries.
- With B22–B26: Challenge Fate 59, Retract Body 39, Nimble Dodge 38; 213 creatures confused (55 snapped out of it by a flat check); 61 Retches.
- Grapple has run at about one a fight since the B6 tuning (3,302 before B7). The first version of this list left it out by mistake.

## PC class features (2026-10-08, the user's choice after B28)

The Foundry snapshot reads a PC's class features through PF2e itself: each Strike's damage is asked for again with a roll option on ("the target is off-guard", "raging"), so the feats and instincts that change it come along. The bestiary tool's benchmark PCs don't have them. **The user confirmed C1–C6 on 2026-10-08, and chose more class features next. The user confirmed C7–C12 the same day, and chose live-play cards next.**

| # | Question | Default chosen | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| C1 | Sneak Attack and other damage against an off-guard target. | The extra dice PF2e gives each Strike against an off-guard target, as precision damage, whenever the target is off-guard to that attack (flanked, prone, grabbed, feinted and so on). PF2e's readable formula drops the word "precision", but damage that only comes against an off-guard target is precision damage. | Read from PF2e, so Sneak Attack's dice by level, its weapon limits and backstabber all come along. |
| C2 | Rage. | 1 action, once a fight: temporary HP equal to level + Constitution modifier, and Rage's damage on the Strikes PF2e gives it to (an instinct's bigger bonus included). While raging, no actions with the concentrate trait (spells, Demoralize, Hunt Prey). It lasts the fight and ends when the barbarian is knocked out. The planner weighs the temporary HP and about three rounds of the extra damage. | PF2e's text; a minute outlasts most fights. |
| C3 | Hunt Prey. | 1 action on the most valuable enemy, and again only when the prey is gone. Flurry edge: against the prey, the multiple attack penalty is −3/−6 (−2/−4 agile). Precision edge: +1d8 precision on the first hit on the prey each round (2d8 from level 11, 3d8 from 19). Outwit edge: +1 AC against the prey; its skill bonuses aren't played. | PF2e's hunter's edges. |
| C4 | Flurry of Blows and Double Slice. | Flurry: 1 action, two Strikes with the monk's best unarmed attack, once a turn. Double Slice: 2 actions, the two best melee weapons in hand (a shield boss counts), at one target, both at the current multiple attack penalty (the second −2 unless agile), counting as two attacks afterwards. Combining the two hits' damage against resistances isn't played. | PF2e's text. |
| C5 | A champion's reaction. | When an enemy damages an ally, with all three in the champion's aura (taken as the same Melee): resistance 2 + the champion's level against that damage. Retributive Strike also Strikes the enemy. Glimpse of Redemption: the enemy repents (the ally takes nothing) when the resistance would stop the damage anyway; otherwise it refuses (resistance, and it's enfeebled 2 until the end of its next turn). Liberating Step's free Escape and Step, and Flash of Grandeur's light, aren't played. Selfish Shield (resistance 2 + half the level, for the champion itself) and Iron Command (the enemy kneels, prone, when the mental damage would be at least a tenth of its HP, otherwise takes it) are read too. | PF2e's causes, with the enemy's choice made for it. |
| C6 | A PC's weapon traits. | Read from PF2e's weapon traits, which the snapshot had missed since the first milestone, so agile weapons now get their smaller multiple attack penalty, and backstabber, sweep, forceful and the like apply. Unarmed attacks count as unarmed, not as weapons in hand. Ancestry traits and weapon traits that change nothing in range bands (parry, trip, disarm and the like) don't count as gaps. | A bug fix the live check found. |
| C7 | Battle Medicine. | 1 action on a hurt ally in reach (the medic's Melee, a downed PC, or itself), once per ally, if the PC carries a healer's toolkit. It uses the Treat Wounds DC its Medicine rank allows that heals most on average: 2d8 HP (4d8 on a critical success) plus 10, 30 or 50 at DC 20, 30 or 40; 1d8 damage on a critical failure. Valued as healing, by urgency. | PF2e's Treat Wounds DCs. |
| C8 | Fighter, barbarian and ranger feats with actions. | Vicious Swing: a melee Strike with an extra weapon die (2 from level 10, 3 from 18), counting as two attacks. Exacting Strike: a plain miss doesn't count toward the multiple attack penalty. Intimidating Strike: frightened 1 on a hit, 2 on a critical hit. Snagging Strike: off-guard until the start of the PC's next turn. Sudden Charge: two Strides and a Strike, once a turn. Hunted Shot and Twin Takedown: two Strikes at the prey, once a turn. Swipe isn't played (two targets at once). | PF2e's text (Knockdown and Power Attack are gone in the remaster). |
| C9 | Exploit Vulnerability (thaumaturge). | 1 action, once a round: an Esoteric Lore check against the target's level DC. A success adds the target's highest weakness or 2 + half the thaumaturge's level (the bigger) to each of its hits on that target, not doubled on a critical hit; a failure, 2 + half level; a critical failure leaves the thaumaturge off-guard until its next turn. It lasts until it exploits another creature. Other creatures of the same kind don't share it. | PF2e's text, the thaumaturge choosing the bigger weakness. |
| C10 | Devise a Stratagem (investigator). | 1 action, once a round, at a creature: its d20 is rolled then, and the first Strike at that creature this round uses it, with Intelligence in place of Strength or Dexterity (agile, finesse and ranged Strikes) and Strategic Strike's precision dice (1d6, +1 at 5, 9, 13, 17). The skill stratagem isn't played. | PF2e's text. |
| C11 | Panache (swashbuckler). | Gained when its style's bravado action succeeds (Feint and Create a Diversion for a fencer, Demoralize for a braggart, Grapple, Shove and Trip for a gymnast); a failed one gives it until the end of the turn. Tumble Through, Bon Mot, Dirty Trick and Perform aren't played, so wits, rascals and battledancers rarely get it. With panache: Precise Strike's damage on agile and finesse melee Strikes; Confident and Unbalancing Finisher spend it (Precise Strike's dice; Confident deals half on a miss; Unbalancing leaves the target off-guard until the end of the PC's next turn), and no more attacks that turn. Opportune Riposte: a Strike back at a foe in reach that critically fails against it. | PF2e's text. |
| C12 | Guardian and inventor. | Intercept Attack: the guardian takes physical damage meant for an ally in its Melee when the ally would drop, or is clearly more hurt. Taunt: an enemy within 30 feet takes −1 to attack anyone else and is off-guard after doing it, until the start of the guardian's next turn. Overdrive: 1 action, once a round until it works, a Crafting check against the inventor's level DC; its Strikes deal its Intelligence modifier more on a critical success, half on a success, 1 fire on a failure; a critical failure burns it (1d6 fire per 2 levels). | PF2e's text. |

**Not yet for PCs:** a magus's Spellstrike, a gunslinger's, commander's and exemplar's features, stances (Tiger Stance, Jotun's Battle Stance), Tumble Through, Bon Mot, and the many other feats with actions.

**Checked live (pftest, 2026-10-08):** with PFTEST SIM copies of the level-5 iconics, every feature above is read and used in fights through the worker. The check also found and fixed a planner error with spell slots alongside a once-only option.

**Auras, 2026-10-08 (with live play's aura cards):** only real auras (PF2e's aura trait or Aura rule) are saved against at the start of a fight, as B2 says. Passives that aren't auras (Death Throes, Defensive Quills) had been played as auras by mistake; they now count as passive abilities. An aura that acts on another trigger (Magic-Warping Aura, when a creature Casts a Spell) or only after another ability (Miasma, after Poison Breath) isn't played at the start either. The bestiary sweep still runs 1,106 creatures with no errors.
