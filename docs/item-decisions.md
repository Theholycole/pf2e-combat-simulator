# Items: decisions

Creatures use their consumables in the simulation through the item module's plans (PF2e Automatic Item Resolution, registered with the resolution core; `src/adapter/items.js`, `src/engine/consumables.js`). Scrolls, wands and staves are spells, S22 in [spell-decisions.md](spell-decisions.md).

| # | Question | Default chosen | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| U1 | When does a creature use its items? (The user, 2026-10-15: "monsters should use their items freely. Players should use rechargeable or reusable items freely but consumables should be weighted to be used when they provide significant more value than other options. Healing items should be used freely if they are projected to go down soon or if they are used on another player that is already down.") | **What's used:** a consumable whose plan the simulation plays: healing, PF2e effects, the item's own conditions on the drinker, and saves and damage on others (potions, elixirs, mutagens, oils, talismans, alchemical tools; 278 of PF2e's consumables in full, 151 in part). Each stack is a pool of its uses; a reusable item (its uses come back) has them all after a rest. Hundreds of uses are a stock's doses, not activations (a black powder keg explodes once).<br>• **How:** its own Activate cost (PF2e's 1 action for a drink when a copy's text has none), and taken out first unless held (PF2e's Interact: 1 worn, more stowed; scrolls and wands too since U6). A manipulate activation provokes. A drink goes to the creature itself or an ally beside it (fed to it); a healing one also to a downed PC, which it reaches as First Aid does.<br>• **Monsters:** freely, at the item's full value.<br>• **PCs:** a reusable item freely. A consumable (and a scroll or a spell gem) at its value divided by a margin of **1.5**: it's used only when it's worth half as much again as the turn's other options. A healing item is used freely when the PC is projected to go down before its next turn (the damage it can expect, each foe's threat shared among the creatures in its Melee, or the whole side, is at least its Hit Points) or fed to a PC who is down. | The user's rule. The margin was measured on the standard party at level 5 with two healing potions and an attack elixir each: elixirs come out 0.17 times a fight in moderate encounters, 0.73 in severe and 1.63 in extreme ones (0.72, 1.17 and 2.05 at no margin; 0.10, 0.33 and 1.07 at 2); with them the party wins 90% of extreme encounters instead of 72%. Not yet: snares, triggered talismans, and the free hand a drink needs. |
| U2 | Bombs (U1's rule for consumables, 2026-10-15): before, a bomb Strike could be thrown without limit. | A bomb, a thrown consumable weapon (a PC's, or the weapon an NPC's Strike links to), is counted by its stack and used up as it's thrown, hit or miss. Before each throw it's taken out (an Interact, as a reload of 1), unless it's in hand or the thrower has Quick Bomber. An alchemist with Quick Alchemy makes more from its infused reagents once its stack is gone (the same action, "makes … with Quick Alchemy"); all of them after a rest. A PC's bomb is weighed as a consumable; a monster's isn't. | PF2e's rules for bombs, Interact and Quick Alchemy. 82 bestiary bomb Strikes link to a weapon with 2 to 4 bombs. Not yet: a Quick Alchemy bomb with no bomb in the inventory (PF2e lists only the inventory's as Strikes), and the best formula's bomb rather than the one carried. |
| U3 | Items that cast a spell (Invisibility Potion, Truesight Potion, Fade Band; the item module's cast-spell plans). | The spell module's plan for the spell (through the core's registry; `spellNamed` in the bestiary tools), cast by the item at its rank, with the item's DC and counteract modifier, else the DC for the item's level. It takes the item's activation (a drink's 1 action), not the spell's casting; it's drunk (the creature or an ally beside it) or, "on you", used on the creature itself. It's an item, not a spell: no Quick Casting, stupefied flat check or counterspell. | PF2e's rule that the item casts the spell. 18 bestiary creatures carry an Invisibility Potion: they drink it and strike from invisibility. Not yet: a spell with a choice of actions (Heal) from an item takes the spell's choices. |
| U4 | Poisons (injury poisons on weapons; ingested and contact poisons, drugs). | An injury poison a creature's Strike lists among its attack effects ("plus lethargy poison", as stat blocks have it) is delivered free by that Strike's hits, as a venom ability is: the poison's save and affliction, a dose a hit, until none are left. Ingested and contact poisons and drugs are left to the table (eaten, touched or taken by their user, not a fight's action). The gap list counts an affliction with stages as played. | The stat blocks' own attack effects. 23 bestiary creatures list a poison they carry (Lethargy Poison on a drow's hand crossbow, Black Adder Venom on a longbow). Snares (set up beforehand, gone after the first creature) are left to the table. | The ability zones' rules. 8 bestiary creatures carry a Smokestick. |

| U10 | Counteract consumables (the user, 2026-10-15: "add the counteract consumables to the simulator"; the item module's `plan.item.counteract`, 28 PF2e consumables). | The item's own counteract check (its modifier and rank, PF2e's counteract rule) on the creature it's used on, against what it names there: a condition a spell or ability gave (its DC and counteract rank as recorded), an effect or condition from a source with one of its traits (emotion, mental, transmutation), an affliction of its kinds (disease, poison, curse: its DC, and half its source creature's level as its rank). "each" tries each, "one" the one worth most, "highest" the highest rank; Moon Radish Soup's success lowers stupefied by 1 (2 on a critical success) instead. An item that only counteracts is drunk or applied (an oil, a salve) by the creature or to an ally beside it; one with a plan of its own (Panacea Fruit's healing, Dragon's Blood Pudding's effect) counteracts beside it. Its value is each counteract's chance times what the thing costs the creature, so it's used only when there's something to end; a PC's is weighed as a consumable. | PF2e's counteract rules; the item module's reading of each item. Checked against bestiary creatures: the Greater salve against Ghoul Razorclaws' paralysis, 8 uses in 12 fights, all counteracted. Not counteracted: a condition from a Strike's own rider or an affliction's stage (no recorded DC). |
