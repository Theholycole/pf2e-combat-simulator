# Feat and action power in combat: save benchmark

The separate save benchmark (Cole, 2026-11-05): the 83 defensive save feats (save bonuses, save rerolls, save reactions), rated against foes that also have save abilities. Every foe gets Venom Spit (Fortitude, poison, sickened), Fire Burst (Reflex, fire, 10-foot burst) and Dread Gaze (Will, emotion, fear, mental, frightened), DC 17 + level, (level/2 + 2)d6 damage, each recharging in 1d4 rounds. Run with `FEAT_FOE_TRAIT=saves node tools/feat-power.mjs <run> ...`. These ratings are a separate column: docs/feat-power.md keeps the normal (Strike-only foes) ratings, where these feats are all about 0.

Made by `node tools/feat-power.mjs` on 2026-11-05: 83 feats and actions rated, 0 not simulated.

## How it's measured

- The feat module compiles each feat with the numbers of the standard party's PC who'd take it (fighter, rogue, wizard or cleric; a class outside the party goes to the one who fights most like it), at the feat's level.
- The party fights a moderate boss (one creature 2 levels up) and a moderate group (four creatures 2 levels down), 200 fights each (at most; a feat's fights stop once its answer is clear: on average 53 an encounter here, no effect 135, all fights 23, precise 5, about zero 3 encounters), with the feat and without it, on the same dice, on a 5-foot square grid (an open field, the sides 30 feet apart, each side's melee fighters in front and its casters two squares behind; moving out of a foe's reach provokes its Reactive Strike). Fights go to the end (no morale, no retreat).
- A fight's score is the danger scale's: the party's Hit Points lost as a share, +0.25 a PC down, +1 a PC killed, +1 a wipe.
- **Rating** is the change in levels of the foes: the score the feat saves, divided by how much the score moves when the foes are a level stronger. +0.25 means having the feat is like fighting foes a quarter of a level lower. The ± is one standard error.
- **Uses a fight** is how often the PC used it in the fights that rated it. A feat the AI never picks rates near zero even if it's good at the table.
- Verdicts: very strong ≥ 0.5, strong ≥ 0.25, good ≥ 0.1, minor > 0; "no clear effect" when within two standard errors of zero.
